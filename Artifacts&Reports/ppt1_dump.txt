01
Journal of Business Logistics (ABDC-A)  •  2023
Machine Learning for Supply Chain Disruption Prediction
Authors: Zhang et al.
Key Finding: XGBoost & LSTM models achieve 87% accuracy in predicting supply disruptions; deep learning outperforms traditional statistical models for non-linear demand patterns.



02
Int. J. of Production Economics (Scopus Q1)  •  2022
Deep Learning Approaches for Demand Forecasting
Authors: Chen & Kumar
Key Finding: LSTM networks reduce MAPE by 34% over ARIMA for SKU-level demand forecasting across multi-echelon supply chains; seasonal patterns captured effectively.



03
Expert Systems with Applications (Scopus Q1)  •  2023
Anomaly Detection in Logistics using Isolation Forest
Authors: Patel et al.
Key Finding: Isolation Forest achieves 91% recall in detecting shipment delays & inventory anomalies; real-time scoring feasible with <50ms latency per event.



04
Supply Chain Mgmt: An Intl. Journal (ABDC-A)  •  2024
AI-Driven Inventory Optimization: A Systematic Review
Authors: Gupta & Singh
Key Finding: Review of 67 studies confirms hybrid ML approaches (ensemble + deep learning) consistently outperform single-model baselines for inventory optimization tasks.

Literature Review — LLMs & AI Assistants in Operations

04 / 12



01
Decision Support Systems (Scopus Q1)  •  2024
GPT-based Decision Support for Operations Management
Authors: Liu et al.
Key Finding: LLM-powered decision assistants reduce analyst decision time by 41%; natural language interfaces improve interpretability of ML predictions significantly.



02
Computers & Industrial Engineering (Scopus Q1)  •  2023
RAG-Enhanced Chatbots for Manufacturing Intelligence
Authors: Sharma & Lee
Key Finding: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over domain corpora improves factual accuracy of LLM responses to operations queries from 61% to 89%.



03
Int. J. of Information Management (ABDC-A)  •  2024
Prompt Engineering for Business Analytics Applications
Authors: Verma et al.
Key Finding: Structured prompt templates with chain-of-thought reasoning outperform zero-shot prompts by 28% on business analytics interpretation benchmarks.

🔍  Research Gap Identified
Existing literature addresses either ML forecasting OR LLM-based interfaces separately. No unified end-to-end platform combining real-time ML predictions, LLM explanation layer, anomaly detection, and interactive dashboards has been proposed for supply chain intelligence — this is the gap this project addresses.
