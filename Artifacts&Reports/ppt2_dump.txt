PROBLEM STATEMENT
02
Modern supply chains are complex, fragile, and data-intensive — yet most organizations still rely on outdated, manual processes that fail to anticipate disruptions.


No Predictive Visibility
Businesses react to disruptions after they happen — stockouts, delays, and demand spikes cause millions in losses.


Data Silos & Fragmentation
Orders, shipments, and inventory exist in separate systems with no unified intelligence layer for analysis.


Manual Decision Making
Operations teams manually analyze spreadsheets and reports, making slow decisions prone to human error.


Lack of Actionable Insights
Even when data exists, there is no AI layer to interpret it, explain root causes, or suggest optimal actions.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES
03
What this project aims to achieve:


01
Unified Data Ingestion
Ingest and unify supply chain data — orders, shipments, and inventory — from multiple sources into a centralized intelligent pipeline.


02
ML-Based Demand Forecasting
Build machine learning models (ARIMA, LSTM, XGBoost) to predict future demand, lead times, and inventory requirements accurately.


03
Real-Time Interactive Dashboard
Develop a React-based dashboard with live charts, KPI cards, and visual analytics for supply chain managers to monitor operations.


04
LLM-Powered AI Assistant
Integrate a Large Language Model (Claude/GPT) to explain predictions in plain language and suggest data-driven operational decisions.


05
Anomaly & Risk Detection
Detect supply chain anomalies — unexpected demand spikes, shipment delays, or inventory shortages — and trigger early-warning alerts.


06
Scalable Cloud Architecture
Design a modular, API-driven architecture deployable on cloud infrastructure with scalability for enterprise-level supply chains.

METHODOLOGY
05


Phase 1
Data Collection & Preprocessing
Gather historical supply chain data. Clean, normalize, and engineer features: lag variables, rolling averages, seasonal indicators.


Phase 2
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Analyze demand patterns, seasonality, and correlations. Visualize trends across product categories, regions, and time periods.


Phase 3
Model Development & Training
Train ARIMA for statistical forecasting, LSTM for sequential patterns, XGBoost for inventory optimization. Evaluate with RMSE, MAPE.


Phase 4
LLM Integration & Prompt Engineering
Connect trained models to Claude/GPT API. Design prompts that convert ML outputs into human-readable business recommendations.


Phase 5
Dashboard Development (React)
Build interactive frontend with real-time charts (Recharts/D3), KPI trackers, alert panels, and the embedded AI assistant chat widget.
